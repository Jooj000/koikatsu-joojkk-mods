# Campus Saves changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-13)
- First version: saves go through a temporary file, every save becomes a version in a vault stored by pieces, saving during the day, autosave with triggers and a timer, panel with import, free space and export.
- The panel takes the place of the game's Load screen: blocks by campus with picture, day and dates, one click loads the latest save, history page with every version, Export saves.
